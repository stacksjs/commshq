# What is CommsHQ

CommsHQ is organized around a team workspace. Every contact, campaign, publication, automation, integration, reputation record, and usage meter is scoped to the active team.

## Product areas

### Audience

Contacts combine email, phone, identities, fields, tags, consent history, and membership in audiences or segments. Import, export, merge, suppression, and data-request records support ongoing data operations.

### Messaging

Email and SMS campaigns use templates and reusable message blocks. Dispatch snapshots eligible recipients, creates idempotent recipient records, and queues delivery work.

### Publishing

Publications, landing pages, link pages, forms, gated resources, podcasts, and episodes provide owned content surfaces tied to the same audience.

### Automation

Versioned automation graphs preserve the exact journey definition used by each run. Step runs, approvals, retries, and dead letters make failures inspectable.

### Commerce and monetization

Signed provider events can record product, cart, checkout, order, refund, and subscription activity. Attribution, entitlements, referrals, sponsor inventory, commissions, earnings, and payouts connect engagement to revenue.

### Reputation

Monitored profiles collect reviews, comments, mentions, and questions. Sentiment, alert rules, and triage state help a team prioritize responses.
