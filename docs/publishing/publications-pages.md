# Publications and pages

A publication is an owned content property scoped to a team. It has a unique slug, description, status, access model, settings, members, podcasts, and referrals.

## Publication state

Publications can be `draft`, `active`, `paused`, or `archived`. Access can be `free`, `paid`, or `mixed`. Activate a publication only after its sender identity, domain, subscription path, and public pages have been tested.

## Landing pages

Landing pages pair focused content with forms, offers, or gated resources. Keep the page purpose clear, use a single primary action, and attribute submissions with the source URL and campaign parameters needed for reporting.

## Link pages

Link pages provide a compact public destination for a creator or publication. Treat every link as published content: validate the target, label it clearly, and remove stale promotions.

## Membership and paid access

Publication membership and entitlements should be derived from explicit subscription or commerce events. Do not grant access based only on a client-side redirect or unverified provider callback.
